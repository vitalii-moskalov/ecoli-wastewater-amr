# Licensing and provenance policy

This is a **mixed-content research repository**. A license for scripts does not automatically grant rights over public genome sequences or external tool/database materials.

1. **Original source code and related software documentation** in `code/` written or otherwise legitimately licensable by the repository contributors: [MIT](LICENSE).
2. **Author-created** figures, tables, curated compilations and research documentation outside `code/`: [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DATA.md), **to the extent the contributors hold the relevant rights**.
3. **Third-party content** — including public NCBI assemblies/metadata, AMRFinderPlus database material, original unmodified reports from external packages where external rights may apply, and quoted material — remains subject to the applicable provider terms and attribution requirements. Neither repository license purports to relicense rights not held by the contributors.
4. **Research claims**: no license conveys a warranty of accuracy, resistance phenotype, confirmed plasmid localization, or horizontal transfer.

Please cite the original NCBI BioProjects PRJNA380388 and PRJNA1266621 when appropriate, and provide the repository commit/release for reproducibility. When the manuscript has a DOI, its full citation should be added here.
